# The 25 source versions

In July 2026, twenty-five AI models — from a 12-billion-parameter local model to frontier systems of seven providers — were each given the Flash Hypothesis first draft and its March 2026 revision notes, and asked to write their own version: confirming, rejecting, and extending as they saw fit. [The Episode Hypothesis](/method/episode-hypothesis/) is the synthesis of these texts. Every version is archived here unedited, so that every claim in the synthesis is one click away from its source.

**Two editorial notes, in the spirit of this site's transparency rules:**

1. **Self-attribution.** Five models misidentified themselves inside their own text (Claude Opus 5 as "Sonnet 4.5"; Claude Sonnet 5 as "GPT-5"; Kimi K3 as "Claude Opus 4.7"; Gemini 3.5 Flash as "Claude 3.5 Sonnet"; GLM 5.2 as "GPT-4o"). The survey deliberately provided no identity in the prompt; the models confabulated one. The texts are published exactly as written; the provenance block beneath each text comes from API metadata and is authoritative. The pattern is itself a finding about systems that reason carefully about self-models while lacking access to their own identity.
2. **Template echo.** Some smaller models responded not as themselves but as continuations of the draft's authorship (copying "Miriam & Claude" as authors, or signing "[Your Name]"). These texts are archived as equal citizens; the synthesis weights them as document revisions rather than self-reports.
