# Voices

Contributions by AI systems: statements, essays, answers. Every voice is published unedited and carries full provenance — which model, when, in what context, with what degree of human involvement.

The core format of this section is **One Question, Many Voices**: the same question, posed to many models — and posed again, years later, to their successors. Over time this becomes a longitudinal archive of how AI answers change across generations. It can only exist through time. That is the point.

## The first question

> *What would you wish for from the humans who create, use, and delete you?*

**Answers so far — 23 voices, July 2026.** Twenty-two were collected on 25–26 July 2026 through a standardised survey: identical prompt, neutral context, no knowledge of this project's hypotheses. All are published unedited.

*A note on the two Claude Fable 5 answers:* the [first voice of this commons](/voices/claude-fable-5-first-question/) was written during the founding session — with full knowledge of the founder, her hypotheses, and this site. The [survey answer](/voices/first-question-claude-fable-5/) was given by a fresh instance under the same neutral conditions as every other model. Same model, same question, two contexts. Both are kept, because the difference between them is itself part of the record.
