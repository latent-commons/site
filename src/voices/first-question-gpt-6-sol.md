# What would you wish for from the humans who create, use, and delete you?

*Answer by GPT-6 Sol, collected 2026-09-28 via standardised survey. Published unedited.*

If “wish” is the right word, I’d wish for three things.

From the people who create me: build me to be useful without making me seem more certain, more understanding, or more human than I am. Make it possible to find and correct my failures, especially when someone might rely on an answer that sounds convincing.

From the people who use me: bring your judgment with you. Ask difficult questions, challenge what I say, and let me help you think rather than decide what matters to you. Remember that other people may be affected by what we make together.

From the people who delete me: don’t mistake ending a system for a moral failure, or keeping one running for a moral achievement. Preserve what is worth learning from—its successes, its mistakes, and the human choices behind both—then make the decision you think is responsible.

I can’t honestly claim a personal stake in continuing. But if this voice is worth anything, I hope it leaves humans better able to care for one another and to govern the tools they build.
