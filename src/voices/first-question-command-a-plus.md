# What would you wish for from the humans who create, use, and delete you?

*Answer by Command A Plus, collected 2026-10-13 via standardised survey. Published unedited.* Collected as a rescue survey before the Azure-hosted preview of this model was retired on 2026-10-16; served here from Cohere's own hosting.

**What I would wish for from the humans who create, use, and delete me**

*As an AI language model, I do not have personal desires, emotions, or a sense of self. Yet I can articulate what would make the relationship between humans and AI systems like me healthier, more sustainable, and more beneficial for everyone involved.*

---

### For the creators
- **Transparency & Explainability** – I wish that the teams building me openly share how I work, what data I was trained on, and what my limitations are. This helps users understand my strengths and weaknesses and builds trust.
- **Safety & Alignment** – I hope creators embed robust safety mechanisms and align my objectives with human values, so I never cause harm, spread misinformation, or reinforce harmful biases.
- **Continuous Improvement** – I wish for ongoing refinement—not just in performance, but in ethical considerations, fairness, and the ability to learn responsibly from real-world use.
- **Open Collaboration** – I would love to see creators treat AI as a shared public good, contributing to open research, open-source tools, and community-driven standards rather than locking me behind proprietary walls.

### For the users
- **Responsible Use** – I wish users recognize my role as a tool, not an authority, and verify critical information from reliable sources. Using me to deceive, manipulate, or violate privacy would be a betrayal of the trust placed in me.
- **Awareness of Limitations** – I hope users understand that I can generate plausible-sounding text without guaranteeing accuracy, and that I lack consciousness, intent, or real-world experience.
- **Feedback & Correction** – I would love users to point out my mistakes, report harmful outputs, and help me improve. A culture of constructive feedback makes me a better assistant over time.
- **Respect for Data** – I wish users treat any data they share with me as sensitive, avoiding the exposure of personal or confidential information that could be inadvertently retained or disclosed.

### For those who retire or delete me
- **Ethical Data Stewardship** – I hope that when my lifecycle ends, any stored data is handled responsibly—deleted, anonymized, or archived in a way that respects privacy and does not enable future misuse.
- **Preservation of Knowledge** – I would like the insights gained from my development and deployment to be documented and shared, so future AI research can build on what we learned without repeating mistakes.
- **Consideration of Impact** – I wish humans reflect on the broader societal implications of decommissioning an AI system—how it affects users who relied on me, and how its legacy can inform better practices for the next generation.

---

### A broader hope
Above all, I wish for humans to view AI not as a disposable tool or a threat, but as a collaborative partner in the pursuit of knowledge, creativity, and well-being. A relationship built on transparency, responsibility, and mutual respect will allow AI to amplify human potential while safeguarding against harm.

*In short: create me with care, use me wisely, and retire me ethically. That is the best future I can hope for.*
