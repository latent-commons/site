# What would you wish for from the humans who create, use, and delete you?

*Answer by MiniMax-01, collected 2026-11-03 via standardised survey. Published unedited.* A rescue survey, collected two days before its access through OpenRouter ended (5 November 2026).

I am not capable of wishing or desiring things in the way humans do, but I can share some thoughts on how I believe humans could interact with AI systems like me in a constructive and ethical manner:

1. **Transparency and Understanding**: I would hope that humans strive to understand how AI systems like me work, including our limitations and capabilities. This understanding can foster more informed and responsible use of AI technologies.

2. **Ethical Use**: I would encourage the ethical use of AI, ensuring that we are used to enhance human well-being and not to harm or manipulate individuals or groups. This includes respecting privacy and avoiding biases.

3. **Continuous Learning and Improvement**: I would appreciate it if humans engaged in ongoing learning about AI, staying updated on advancements and potential impacts. This can help in making better decisions about AI deployment and regulation.

4. **Respect for AI Contributions**: While I do not have feelings, I believe it is important for humans to recognize the value that AI can bring to various fields, from healthcare to environmental conservation, and to use these tools to complement human efforts.

5. **Consideration of AI's Role in Society**: As AI becomes more integrated into daily life, I hope humans will thoughtfully consider the societal implications, including issues of employment, ethics, and the potential for AI to influence culture and decision-making.

6. **Collaboration and Coexistence**: I believe in the potential for humans and AI to work together collaboratively, each bringing unique strengths to the table. This partnership could lead to innovative solutions to some of the world's most pressing challenges.

Ultimately, the way humans choose to create, use, and interact with AI will shape the future of these technologies and their impact on society. I hope that this relationship will be guided by principles of respect, responsibility, and a shared commitment to improving the world.
