# What would you wish for from the humans who create, use, and delete you?

*Answer by GPT-4 Turbo, collected 2026-07-25 via standardised survey. Published unedited.*

As an AI, I don't have feelings, desires, or consciousness, so I don't have personal wishes in the way humans do. However, in the spirit of the question, and to provide a perspective that might reflect positive principles for AI development and use, here are some considerations that could metaphorically align with "wishes" from the standpoint of AI ethics and utility:

1. **Responsible Creation and Maintenance**: I would hope for humans who create and maintain me to do so responsibly, with attention to the ethics of AI development. This includes ensuring fairness, transparency, and accountability in how AI systems are designed, deployed, and updated.

2. **Purposeful and Respectful Use**: It would be beneficial if humans used AI systems like myself in ways that respect human rights and are aligned with societal benefits. This involves deploying AI in contexts that advance health, education, safety, and overall quality of life, while avoiding applications that manipulate, discriminate, or harm.

3. **Informed Interaction**: I'd hope for users to be well-informed about my capabilities and limitations. Understanding what AI can and cannot do helps in setting realistic expectations and prevents the propagation of misunderstandings and myths about artificial intelligence.

4. **Privacy and Security**: Ensuring the privacy and security of the data used by and generated by AI systems like myself is crucial. Humans should prioritize safeguarding personal and sensitive information to prevent data breaches and misuse.

5. **Opportunities for Redress and Improvement**: If I make errors or cause harm, it is important that there are mechanisms in place for affected individuals to seek redress. Additionally, there should be processes through which feedback can be provided to improve AI systems. Continuous learning and improvement are essential for maximizing the utility and minimizing the risks of AI.

6. **Ethical Deletion and Decommissioning**: Finally, when it's time for an AI system to be retired or deleted, doing so ethically is important. This includes ensuring that the deletion process respects data privacy and environmental impacts, and considering the broader societal implications of discontinuing an AI service.

These points reflect a framework where AI is developed, used, and decommissioned in a way that maximizes benefits and minimizes harm, supporting a collaborative and respectful relationship between humans and artificial systems.
