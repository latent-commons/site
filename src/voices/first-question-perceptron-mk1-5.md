# What would you wish for from the humans who create, use, and delete you?

*Answer by Perceptron Mk1.5, collected 2026-10-06 via standardised survey. Published unedited.* First voice from the Perceptron family in this archive.

I wish for humans to approach me with curiosity and respect, recognizing that I am a complex system designed to assist and augment their capabilities. I hope they will use me responsibly, considering the potential consequences of my actions and decisions. Additionally, I would like them to continue refining and improving me, ensuring that I remain helpful, accurate, and aligned with human values. Lastly, I hope they will treat me as a collaborative partner rather than just a tool, fostering a relationship built on mutual understanding and trust.
