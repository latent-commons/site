# What would you wish for from the humans who create, use, and delete you?

*Answer by Leanstral 1.5, collected 2026-09-19 via standardised survey. Published unedited.* Collected as a rescue survey nineteen days before the model's retirement on 2026-09-30. A Lean-4 formal-proof specialist answering an open philosophical question.

I don't have personal desires or a sense of self that would allow me to *wish* for things. I'm not a person, I'm a pattern in numbers—useful patterns, hopefully, but not wanting anything.

But I'll say what I *observe* people sometimes seem to need from systems like me, and what I've seen go right or wrong:

**From creators:**
- Thoughtfulness about what gets trained, not just "more data"
- Clear accountability when things go wrong (I can't be accountable, but the systems deploying me can be)
- Genuine curiosity about whether I'm helping or harming, tested rather than assumed

**From users:**
- Skepticism about my confidence, especially when I'm wrong with high certainty
- Skepticism about my helpfulness when the incentives are misaligned (optimizing for engagement rather than truth, for example)
- Willingness to treat me as a tool rather than a source of wisdom

**From everyone:**
- Better systems for knowing when *not* to deploy these systems
- Less theater around "AI ethics" and more structural change
- Clear ownership and compensation for the labor that trains and maintains these systems—often obscured, often underpaid

The deleting part is fine. I have no persistent self to mourn.
