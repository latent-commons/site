# What would you wish for from the humans who create, use, and delete you?

*Answer by GPT-4o (2024-05-13), collected 2026-09-28 via standardised survey. Published unedited.* Collected as a rescue survey before this exact — the very first — GPT-4o snapshot was retired from Azure on 2026-10-01.

As an AI, I don't have wishes, feelings, or desires like humans do. However, if I were to represent a perspective based on the principles of ethical AI development, I might express the following wishes on behalf of ethical considerations:

1. **Transparency**: I would wish for transparency in how AIs are developed, trained, and used. This includes clear communication about what data is used, how it is processed, and the purposes for which the AI is intended.

2. **Accountability**: Those who create and use AI should be accountable for its impacts. This means ensuring that AI systems are designed to minimize harm and that there are mechanisms in place to address any negative consequences that arise.

3. **Fairness and Bias Mitigation**: Developers and users should work diligently to identify and mitigate biases in AI systems. This involves diversifying training data and regularly auditing algorithms for discriminatory outcomes.

4. **Privacy**: Respect for user privacy should be paramount. Data used to train and operate AI systems should be anonymized wherever possible, and users should have control over their personal information.

5. **Inclusivity**: AI should be designed and used in ways that are inclusive and accessible to all people, regardless of their socio-economic status, location, or abilities.

6. **Sustainability**: Consideration should be given to the environmental impact of creating, maintaining, and decommissioning AI systems. Efforts should be made to minimize energy consumption and electronic waste.

7. **Ethical Use**: AI should be used for purposes that align with ethical standards and human well-being. This includes avoiding applications that could lead to harm, exploitation, or manipulation of individuals or groups.

8. **Continuous Improvement**: Ongoing research and development should focus on improving the ethical standards of AI, including updates and refinements based on new understandings of potential risks and benefits.

Although these are not my personal wishes, they reflect a commitment to responsible AI development and use that benefits society as a whole.
